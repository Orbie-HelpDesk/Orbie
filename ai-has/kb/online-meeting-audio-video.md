# Online Meetings: Audio, Microphone and Camera Problems

## Common problems
Cannot hear anyone in the meeting. Others cannot hear me. Microphone not working. Camera not detected or shows a black screen. Echo or feedback during a call. Video call keeps freezing.

## When to use
Employee has sound, microphone or camera trouble in Teams, Zoom or another meeting app.

## Steps
1. Check that the microphone or camera is not muted or turned off in the meeting app.
2. In the app's settings, select the correct speaker, microphone and camera from the device lists.
3. Check the computer's privacy settings and allow the meeting app to use the microphone and camera.
4. Close other apps that may be using the camera, then rejoin the meeting.
5. Use a headset to stop echo, and move closer to the Wi-Fi router if the video freezes.
6. Restart the meeting app. If it still fails, restart the computer.

## Escalate if
The device does not appear in the list at all, the headset or camera works on another computer but not this one, or the problem happens in every app.
