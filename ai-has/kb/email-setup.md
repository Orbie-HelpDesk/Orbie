# Setting Up Work Email on a Phone

## Common problems
How do I set up my work email on my phone? Add company email to my phone. Outlook app setup. Email is not working on my phone. Cannot sign in to email on mobile.

## Steps
1. Install the company email app (Outlook) from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app and tap "Add account".
3. Enter your work email address and tap "Continue".
4. Sign in with your company password and approve the authenticator prompt.
5. Accept the device security policy when asked.

## Escalate if
Sign-in fails after several attempts, or the security policy cannot be installed on your device.
