# Password Reset (Company Account)

## When to use
Employee forgot their password or the account is locked after failed attempts.

## Steps
1. Go to the self-service portal and click "Forgot password".
2. Verify identity using the registered email or authenticator app.
3. Set a new password (min. 12 characters, 1 number, 1 symbol).
4. Wait up to 5 minutes for sync, then sign in again.

## Escalate if
Verification fails, the account is disabled, or the user has no access to their registered email.
