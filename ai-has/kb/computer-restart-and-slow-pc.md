# Restarting a Computer and Fixing a Slow PC

## Common problems
How can I restart my computer? My computer is slow. My PC is laggy. My laptop is freezing or hanging. Computer takes too long to start. Computer is not responding.

## How to restart a Windows computer
1. Save your work and close all open applications.
2. Click the Start menu, then the Power button.
3. Choose "Restart" (not "Shut down").
4. Wait for the computer to boot and sign in again.

## If the computer is slow
1. Restart the computer. This fixes most slowdowns.
2. Close programs and browser tabs you are not using.
3. Check for pending Windows updates and install them.
4. Make sure at least 15 percent of the disk space is free.

## Escalate if
The computer is frozen and will not respond, restarts by itself repeatedly, or is still slow after a restart and cleanup.
