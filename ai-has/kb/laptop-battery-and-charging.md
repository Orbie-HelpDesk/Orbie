# Laptop Battery and Charging Problems

## Common problems
Laptop will not charge. Battery drains very fast. Plugged in but not charging. Charger gets hot. Laptop shuts down when unplugged. Laptop will not turn on.

## When to use
Employee has trouble charging a laptop or the battery does not last.

## Steps
1. Check the power outlet with another device, and plug the charger firmly into the laptop and the wall.
2. Look for a charging light on the charger or the laptop.
3. Remove other attachments such as docks and USB devices, then try charging again.
4. Restart the laptop, and lower the screen brightness and close unused apps to save battery.
5. Only use the charger that came with the laptop or one approved by IT.
6. If the laptop will not turn on, hold the power button for 15 seconds, then connect the charger and wait 10 minutes before turning it on.

## Escalate if
The charger or port is damaged or very hot, the battery is swollen, or the laptop still does not charge with a known good charger. Stop using a swollen battery immediately.
