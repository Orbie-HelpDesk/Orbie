# Printer Troubleshooting

## Common problems
My printer is not working. Printer won't print. Printer is offline. Document is stuck in the print queue. Printer shows an error. Cannot print from my computer. Paper jam.

## Common fixes
1. Check that the printer is on and shows no error light.
2. Confirm the paper tray has paper and there is no paper jam.
3. Clear the print queue: open the printer queue, cancel all documents, and print again.
4. Make sure you selected the correct printer in the print window.
5. Restart the printer and try again.

## Escalate if
The printer does not appear in your list, shows a hardware error, or needs toner replacement.
