# Phishing and Suspicious Emails

## Common problems
I got a strange email. Email asks for my password. Suspicious link or attachment. I clicked a link in a fake email. Email pretending to be the CEO or IT. Spam or scam message.

## When to use
Employee received or interacted with an email that looks like phishing.

## Steps
1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply to the message.
2. Never give your password, MFA code or personal details by email. IT will never ask for them.
3. Report the email using the "Report phishing" button in the mail app, or forward it to the IT helpdesk.
4. Delete the email after reporting it.
5. If you already clicked a link or typed your password, change your password right away from a trusted device and tell the IT helpdesk.

## Escalate if
You entered your password or code on a suspicious page, opened an attachment, or see strange activity in your account. Treat this as a security incident and report it immediately.
