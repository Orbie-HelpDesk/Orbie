# Lost or Stolen Laptop or Phone

## Common problems
I lost my laptop. My phone was stolen. Left my work device on the bus. Cannot find my company device. Someone took my computer.

## When to use
A company laptop, phone or tablet is lost or stolen, or a personal phone with work email is lost.

## Steps
1. Report it to the IT helpdesk immediately, even if you are not sure it is lost.
2. Give the device type, when and where you last had it, and whether it was locked.
3. IT can lock the device and remotely wipe company data. Do not wait to see if it turns up.
4. Change your password from another trusted device.
5. If it was stolen, also report it to building security or the police as required by company policy.

## Escalate if
The device held sensitive data, the report is delayed more than a few hours, or you see sign-in attempts you do not recognize. This is always treated as high priority.
