# Software Access Request

## Common problems
I need access to Figma. I need Slack, Jira, Microsoft Office or Adobe. How do I get a software license? Request an application. I cannot open an app because I have no access.

## When to use
Employee needs access to a work application such as Figma, Slack, Jira, Microsoft Office, or Adobe tools.

## Steps
1. Open the self-service portal and go to "Software Requests".
2. Select the application and enter a short business reason.
3. Submit the request. Your manager receives an approval notification.
4. After approval, IT provisions the account within 1 business day.
5. Check your work email for the invitation or login instructions.

## Escalate if
The request is urgent, the application is not listed in the portal, or approval takes more than 2 business days.
