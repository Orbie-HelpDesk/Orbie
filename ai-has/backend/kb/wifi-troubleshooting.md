# Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

## Common problems
Wi-Fi is not working. No internet connection. Internet not working. Cannot open websites. Cannot connect to the Wi-Fi. Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting or dropping. Internet is slow. Office network is not showing.

## Common fixes
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again from the taskbar.
2. Forget the office network, then reconnect and sign in with your company account.
3. Restart your computer.
4. Move closer to the access point and check if other devices connect.
5. Run the Windows network troubleshooter from Settings.

## Escalate if
Several people in the same area have no connection, or the network does not appear in the list at all.
